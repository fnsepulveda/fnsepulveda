# Felipe Sepúlveda

**PhD Candidate in Economics** · [Vancouver School of Economics](https://economics.ubc.ca), University of British Columbia
PhD Fellow (2026–2028) at the [Stone Centre on Wealth and Income Inequality](https://stonecentre.economics.ubc.ca/)

Welcome! I work on **urban and environmental economics** — housing policy, the spatial structure of cities, and how climate shocks affect households and markets. Before the PhD I earned my B.A. and M.A. in Economics at [Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile](https://www.uc.cl) and spent five years in economic consulting (litigation, antitrust, and public policy) at [FK Economics](https://www.fkeconomics.com).
